# Parties and Transaction Structure

**A.** Adrian Lipa of \[SELLER ADDRESS\] (Seller).

**B.** \[BUYER LEGAL NAME\], a company incorporated in \[JURISDICTION\] with company number \[●\] and registered office at \[●\] (Buyer).

**C.** The Seller agrees to sell only the Assigned DNCrypt Assets expressly identified in Schedule 1. The Excluded Assets, Retained Projects, Background IP and Retained Interoperability Assets are not sold.

**D.** The parties intend that no title passes until Completion and receipt of the full Purchase Price, and that the Seller retains permanent freedom to continue the Retained Projects under the licence-back and non-assert protections below.

**E.** Each party confirms that it is acting wholly for the purposes of its business and not as a consumer.

**1. Definitions**

**"Assigned DNCrypt Assets"** means only the DNCrypt-specific assets expressly listed in Schedule 1, as fixed by an immutable commit/archive hash and exact asset schedule.

**"Assigned IP"** means copyright, database rights, DNCrypt-specific confidential know-how/trade secrets, and any patent or patent application only if expressly identified in Schedule 1, in each case to the extent owned by the Seller and comprised in the Assigned DNCrypt Assets.

**"Background IP"** means all intellectual property, code, algorithms, mathematical methods, architectures, protocols, know-how, research, tools and materials owned or controlled by the Seller outside the exact Assigned DNCrypt Assets or independently usable outside DNCrypt.

**"Excluded Assets"** means all assets listed in Schedule 2 and anything not expressly listed in Schedule 1.

**"Retained Projects"** means the projects listed in Schedule 2 and their successors, forks, modules and independently developed replacements.

**"Retained Interoperability Assets"** means the interface specifications, schemas, wire formats, field semantics, conformance tests/test vectors, adapters and other interoperability materials listed in Schedule 2, together with independent implementations of them.

**"Completion"** means the completion of the sale under clause 5 after all Completion Conditions are satisfied or waived and the full cleared Purchase Price has been received.

**2. Sale and purchase**

**2.1** At Completion, the Seller sells and assigns to the Buyer, and the Buyer purchases, the Assigned DNCrypt Assets and Assigned IP only.

**2.2** No asset, right, repository, codebase, account, project, algorithm, know-how or intellectual property passes by implication. If an item is not expressly identified in Schedule 1, it remains with the Seller.

**2.3** Third-Party Components are not sold. The Buyer receives only such rights as arise under the applicable third-party licences.

**2.4** No future version, future improvement, future invention or future work of the Seller is sold unless a later written agreement signed by the Seller expressly assigns it.

**3. Expressly Excluded and Retained Assets**

**3.1** The Excluded Assets and Retained Projects remain the sole property of the Seller and are unaffected by this Agreement.

**3.2** Without limitation, the Buyer acquires no right to control, restrict, approve, veto, acquire, license exclusively, encumber or claim ownership of any Retained Project, Background IP or Retained Interoperability Asset.

**3.3** The Seller may continue to research, develop, use, publish, license and commercialise the Retained Projects and independently developed cryptographic, adaptive-security, state-memory, file-format, coding-system and interoperability technology, including technology that competes with or interoperates with DNCrypt.

**3.4** The sale imposes no non-compete, non-development or research restriction on the Seller except that the Seller shall not falsely represent ownership of the specific Assigned DNCrypt Assets after Completion.

**4. Purchase Price, deposit and taxes**

**4.1** The Purchase Price is GBP \[●\], payable as stated in Schedule 3.

**4.2** Unless Schedule 3 states otherwise, no title or licence beyond pre-Completion due-diligence access passes until the Seller has received 100% of the Purchase Price in cleared funds.

**4.3** Any deposit is applied to the Purchase Price and is refundable or non-refundable only as expressly stated in Schedule 3.

**4.4** The Purchase Price is exclusive of VAT if VAT is lawfully chargeable.

**4.5** Each party bears its own professional fees and taxes except where this Agreement expressly states otherwise.

**5. Completion Conditions and Completion**

**5.1** Completion is conditional on: (a) cleared payment of the Purchase Price; (b) execution of this Agreement; (c) completion of the agreed asset schedule and hash verification; (d) required export-control/sanctions authorisations; and (e) any agreed National Security and Investment Act or other investment-screening clearance.

**5.2** If a regulatory authorisation is required, neither party is required to commit an unlawful transfer. The parties shall cooperate reasonably on lawful notifications or licence applications.

**5.3** At Completion, the Seller shall deliver the items in Schedule 4 using a mutually agreed secure transfer method.

**5.4** The Buyer shall issue a written delivery receipt confirming the hashes of delivered materials. A delivery receipt does not waive latent title claims or fraud, but confirms the identity of the delivered snapshot.

**6. Assignment of copyright and other Assigned IP**

**6.1** With effect only from Completion, the Seller assigns to the Buyer with full title guarantee, to the extent owned by the Seller, the copyright and other Assigned IP in the Assigned DNCrypt Assets for the remaining duration of those rights, subject always to clauses 7 and 8.

**6.2** The assignment is limited to the assets and rights identified in Schedule 1. It does not include Background IP, Excluded Assets, Retained Interoperability Assets or future works.

**6.3** If Schedule 1 includes a specifically identified patent or patent application, the parties shall execute any additional recordal form reasonably required to register the assignment. No patent right is transferred merely by general wording.

**6.4** Moral rights, to the extent they exist and may lawfully be waived, are waived only in relation to the Assigned DNCrypt Assets and only to the extent reasonably necessary for the Buyer to exploit them. No waiver applies to Excluded Assets or Retained Projects.

**7. Perpetual Retained-Project licence-back**

**7.1** At Completion, the Buyer grants the Seller a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, non-exclusive licence under the Assigned IP to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, compile, execute, create derivative works from, distribute, make available and otherwise exploit those parts of the Assigned DNCrypt Assets that are reasonably required for the continued development, testing, operation, support, interoperability and commercialisation of the Retained Projects.

**7.2** The licence-back includes the right to sublicense such rights to contributors, contractors, distributors, hosting providers, customers and users of Retained Projects where reasonably necessary to exercise clause 7.1.

**7.3** The licence-back permits commercial Retained Projects and independent products, even if they compete with DNCrypt, but does not authorise the Seller to market an unmodified or substantially complete copy of the exact Assigned DNCrypt product as 'DNCrypt' under any Buyer-owned DNCrypt trademark.

**7.4** The licence-back is an encumbrance that the Buyer accepts as part of the transaction and survives any sale, assignment, insolvency, corporate reorganisation, change of control or onward transfer of the Assigned IP.

**7.5** The Buyer shall ensure that any transferee of Assigned IP takes it expressly subject to this licence-back and the non-assert covenant in clause 8.

**8. Retained interoperability, patent covenant and non-assert**

**8.1** Retained Interoperability Assets are Excluded Assets and are not assigned. The Seller retains unrestricted rights to implement, publish, modify and license them independently.

**8.2** The Buyer shall not assert Assigned IP or Buyer-controlled rights against the Seller, a Retained Project, or their contributors, users, contractors or licensees solely because they implement a Retained Interoperability Asset, exercise the licence-back, or interoperate with DNCrypt.

**8.3** If the Buyer or an Affiliate owns or controls a patent claim, utility model or equivalent right that would necessarily be infringed by implementing a Retained Interoperability Asset or exercising the licence-back, the Buyer grants the Seller a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable and sublicensable licence under that claim/right for Retained Projects.

**8.4** The Buyer shall not register or assert any security interest, contractual restriction or technical access mechanism intended to prevent the Seller from exercising clauses 7 or 8.

**9. Repositories, accounts, secrets and operational access**

**9.1** The Seller's GitHub account, email accounts, cloud accounts, NOEMA/CIEL environments, keys, credentials, API tokens, private signing keys and all repositories other than the exact DNCrypt delivery repository/snapshot are Excluded Assets.

**9.2** The Seller shall not deliver live secrets, private keys or unrelated credentials. Where necessary, the Buyer shall create its own credentials and infrastructure.

**9.3** If a repository is transferred, the transfer shall be limited to the DNCrypt repository expressly identified in Schedule 1 and shall occur only at Completion. The Seller may retain an archival copy for legal, provenance and licence-back purposes.

**10. Confidentiality and due diligence**

**10.1** Pre-Completion disclosures are for due diligence only and remain confidential. No pre-Completion disclosure grants ownership or an exploitation licence.

**10.2** The Buyer shall restrict source-code access to personnel and advisers who need it and are bound by confidentiality.

**10.3** If Completion does not occur, the Buyer shall cease use and return or securely delete non-public DNCrypt materials, subject to one legal archive copy held inaccessible in the ordinary course.

**10.4** Trade-secret confidentiality survives for so long as the information remains a trade secret; other confidentiality obligations survive for five years.

**11. Security status and Buyer's independent evaluation**

**11.1** The Buyer acknowledges that DNCrypt is security-sensitive software and that internal tests, controlled simulations, receipts, holdouts and conformance results are evidence of the stated test scope only and are not an independent security certification or guarantee of cryptographic security.

**11.2** The Buyer is responsible for independent cryptanalysis, security review, side-channel assessment, key-management review, integration testing and regulatory assessment before production or high-impact deployment.

**11.3** The Buyer shall not market internal evidence as an independent certification or attribute a security guarantee to the Seller without the Seller's prior written approval.

**12. Seller warranties**

**12.1** The Seller warrants that, to the Seller's knowledge at signing and Completion: (a) the Seller is entitled to sell the Assigned DNCrypt Assets identified in Schedule 1; (b) the Seller has not knowingly granted an undisclosed conflicting exclusive assignment of those same assets; and (c) the Seller has not knowingly inserted malicious code or a deliberate undisclosed backdoor into the delivered snapshot.

**12.2** The Seller does not warrant that DNCrypt is error-free, vulnerability-free, patentable, free from all third-party claims, fit for a particular purpose, compatible with every system or secure against every current or future cryptanalytic attack.

**12.3** The Buyer confirms it has had the opportunity to inspect the source, evidence, provenance, third-party components and technical status and is purchasing subject to the express limitations of this Agreement.

**13. Third-party rights and open-source components**

**13.1** Third-Party Components remain governed by their own licences and are not owned or assigned by the Seller.

**13.2** The Buyer shall comply with those licences and is responsible for its post-Completion distribution model.

**13.3** The Buyer acknowledges that the use of public cryptographic standards or algorithms does not itself constitute ownership of those standards or algorithms by the Seller.

**14. Liability and claims**

**14.1** Nothing excludes or limits liability for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

**14.2** Subject to clause 14.1, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, revenue, goodwill, anticipated savings or business opportunity.

**14.3** Subject to clause 14.1, the Seller's aggregate liability under this Agreement, including for breach of warranty, shall not exceed 100% of the Purchase Price actually received.

**14.4** The Buyer shall notify a warranty claim promptly after discovery and in any event within 18 months after Completion, except for fraud or a title claim that cannot lawfully be time-limited by contract.

**14.5** The limitations in this clause are intended to be reasonable in a negotiated business-to-business sale following technical due diligence.

**15. Buyer indemnity after Completion**

**15.1** The Buyer shall indemnify the Seller against third-party claims, penalties and reasonable external costs arising from the Buyer's post-Completion distribution, deployment, modification, misleading security claims, unlawful export, sanctions breach or regulated use of the Assigned DNCrypt Assets, except to the extent caused by the Seller's fraud or breach of clause 12.1.

**16. Export controls, sanctions and NSI**

**16.1** The parties shall comply with applicable UK and other export-control, sanctions and trade-control laws. Controlled cryptographic software, technology, data or technical assistance shall not be transferred without required authorisation.

**16.2** The parties acknowledge that acquisitions of control over intangible assets can be within the call-in power under the National Security and Investment Act 2021. If the transaction may reasonably raise a national-security screening issue, the parties shall obtain advice and cooperate on an appropriate voluntary notification or other clearance before Completion.

**16.3** A party may delay Completion to the extent reasonably necessary to obtain a required licence or clearance, and neither party is obliged to make an unlawful transfer.

**17. Transition assistance**

**17.1** No continuing employment, consultancy, support or development obligation is included unless Schedule 3 states otherwise.

**17.2** Any transition services shall be documented separately with hours, rates, scope, confidentiality and ownership terms.

**18. Names, marks and publicity**

**18.1** The DNCrypt name, logo, domain and associated marks transfer only if expressly listed in Schedule 1.

**18.2** Neither party may publicly announce the transaction or use the other party's name or logo without prior written approval, except as required by law or a regulator.

**18.3** The Buyer shall not imply that the Seller personally endorses post-Completion modifications, security claims or deployments.

**19. Further assurance**

**19.1** After Completion, each party shall sign reasonable documents necessary to give effect to the express assignment, retained licence-back and retained interoperability protections, provided no document expands the assets sold or diminishes the Seller's reserved rights.

**19.2** If a recordal authority requires a short-form assignment, it shall state that it is subject to this Agreement, including clauses 7 and 8.

**20. General**

**20.1** This Agreement and its Schedules constitute the entire agreement concerning the sale and supersede prior discussions on that subject.

**20.2** No amendment, waiver of a retained right, expansion of Schedule 1 or removal of a licence-back protection is effective unless in writing signed by the Seller and the Buyer and expressly identifying the clause changed.

**20.3** If a provision is invalid or unenforceable it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary, and the rest remains effective.

**20.4** No failure or delay to exercise a right is a waiver.

**20.5** No partnership, agency, fiduciary relationship, employment or joint venture is created.

**20.6** Except that persons within the class of protected Retained Project contributors, users, contractors and licensees may rely on clause 8 where expressly intended, no other person has a right to enforce this Agreement under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

**20.7** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature.

**20.8** Notices shall be sent to the contact details in Schedule 3.

**21. Governing law and jurisdiction**

**21.1** This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising from it are governed by the law of England and Wales.

**21.2** The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction, without preventing urgent injunctive relief in another competent jurisdiction to preserve confidentiality, the retained licence-back or intellectual property.
