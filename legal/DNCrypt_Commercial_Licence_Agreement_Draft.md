**"DNCrypt IP"** means copyright, database rights, design rights (if any), DNCrypt-specific confidential know-how and trade secrets subsisting in the Licensed Software, plus any patent or patent application only if expressly listed in Schedule 1.

**"Retained Projects"** means the projects listed in Schedule 3, and their successors, forks, modules and independently developed replacements.

**"Retained Interoperability Assets"** means interface specifications, schemas, wire formats, field semantics, protocol definitions, conformance tests/test vectors, adapters and other interoperability materials identified in Schedule 3 or reasonably required to permit independent interoperability between DNCrypt and a Retained Project.

**"Third-Party Components"** means software, standards, libraries and materials owned by third parties, including open-source software, listed or referred to in Schedule 4.

**"Field"** means the commercial field (if any) stated in Schedule 2. If no Field is stated, the licence is non-exclusive and not field-limited.

**3.2** The Licensor may continue to research, develop, use, license, publish and commercialise the Retained Projects, cryptographic systems, adaptive-security systems, memory systems, file formats, protocols and independently developed technology, including technology that competes with or interoperates with the Licensed Software.

**4. Retained interoperability and anti-blocking covenant**

**4.1** The Retained Interoperability Assets remain owned by or freely implementable by the Licensor. The Licensee receives only the interoperability rights required for the Licensed Software.

**4.2** The Licensee shall not assert any DNCrypt IP, Licensee improvement right or Licensee-controlled patent against the Licensor, a Retained Project, or their users, contractors or licensees solely because they implement a Retained Interoperability Asset or interoperate with DNCrypt.

**4.3** To the extent a Licensee-controlled patent claim or other improvement right would necessarily be infringed by implementing a Retained Interoperability Asset or by continuing a Retained Project, the Licensee grants the Licensor a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable and sublicensable licence to that claim/right for the development, operation, distribution and commercialisation of Retained Projects.

**4.4** The protections in this clause bind permitted assignees and successors of the Licensee. The Licensee shall not transfer relevant rights unless the transferee takes them expressly subject to this clause.

**5.1** The Licensor retains title to the Licensed Software and DNCrypt IP. The Licensee owns code or materials independently created by it that do not contain or derive from Licensor Confidential Information or DNCrypt source code.

**5.3** Nothing in this Agreement assigns future inventions, future DNCrypt versions or future work of the Licensor.

**6.1** Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own information of similar importance and, in any event, reasonable care.

**6.3** The confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is lawfully public, already lawfully known without restriction, independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or lawfully received from a third party without duty of confidence.

**6.5** Trade-secret obligations continue for so long as the information remains a trade secret; other confidentiality obligations continue for five years after termination.
